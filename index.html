Questions

Everything people ask
before they start

Why is there no pricing on this website?
Because a fixed price list would be dishonest. A five-page brochure site and a platform with logins, a database and an admin panel are not the same job, and quoting them from the same table means someone gets overcharged. Send us the scope through the form or on WhatsApp and you will get one straight number for exactly what you need — usually the same day.
How do I actually start?
Two routes, both land in the same place. Message the WhatsApp number and talk it through, or fill the project brief at the bottom of this page. The form validates everything and opens WhatsApp with your details already written out, so it reaches Hashir directly rather than sitting in an inbox.
How long does a project take?
A focused landing page is usually 3–6 days. A full multi-page business site runs 1–3 weeks. Anything with a backend — logins, databases, admin panels — is typically 3–5 weeks. Reels and Shorts turn around in 24–72 hours. Urgent work can be prioritised if you tell us up front.
Do I own the website when it is finished?
Completely. It deploys to your own domain and your own hosting account, and you get every file. There is no platform lock-in, no monthly ransom to keep the site online, and no situation where you need our permission to move it somewhere else.
Do you use WordPress or a page builder?
No. Everything is hand-written. Builders add weight that shows up as slow load times on mobile, they limit what can be built once you outgrow the template, and they age badly. Custom code costs about the same at our level and performs in a different league.
Can you handle the website, branding, video and ads together?
That is the main reason clients come to us rather than assembling three freelancers. When the same team writes the site, designs the logo, cuts the video and runs the campaigns, the brand actually looks like one brand, and nothing gets lost in handovers between people who have never spoken.
What exactly is an AI calling agent?
A voice agent that makes and takes phone calls for your business. It rings new leads within minutes of them enquiring, qualifies them with real conversation, books appointments into your calendar, sends reminders and chases the ones who went quiet. Every call is transcribed and logged so you can see what was said.
Do you work with clients outside Karachi?
Yes — across Pakistan and internationally. Everything runs remotely and always has. Meetings happen on WhatsApp, a call or Google Meet, and the work is shared through live preview links as it is built.
