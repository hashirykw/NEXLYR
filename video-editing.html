Straight answers

Before you send the first file.

How fast is the turnaround?
A reel or short is 24 to 72 hours. A long-form YouTube edit is three to six days depending on length and how much footage there is. A multicam podcast is usually four to seven. If something is urgent, say so up front and it gets prioritised rather than quietly queued.
Does phone footage work?
Yes, and most of what we cut starts that way. Shoot in the highest setting your phone offers, keep it steady, and record somewhere quiet — bad audio is the one thing an edit cannot fully rescue. Everything else we can work with.
How many revisions do I get?
One full round is included on every edit, and because you approve the rough cut before finishing starts, that round is almost always small. Structural changes after approval count as a new edit, which is exactly why the rough cut stage exists.
Do you write the hook and captions too?
Yes. The on-screen hook, the burned-in captions and the caption for the post itself, if you want them. It is usually better that way — the person cutting the video already knows where the strongest line is.
Why is there no price list?
A thirty-second reel from clean footage and a twenty-minute multicam podcast are not the same job. Send the footage or describe it on WhatsApp and you get one straight number for exactly that, usually the same day.
