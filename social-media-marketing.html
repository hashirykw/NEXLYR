Straight answers

The questions we get asked first.

Do I need to be on camera?
No. Plenty of accounts we run never show a face — carousels, screen recordings, product and process footage, text-led reels. If you are willing to be on camera it usually helps, but it is not the price of entry.
Who films the content?
Either you send raw footage from your phone and we do everything after that, or we shoot on site in Karachi. Most clients start with the first and move to the second once they see what a proper shoot day produces.
Is the ad budget included?
No — you pay Meta directly from your own ad account, so you keep the pixel, the data and the history. We are paid for the work, not a slice of your spend, which means there is no incentive for us to tell you to spend more than you should.
How fast will I see something?
The calendar lands in the first week and posting starts in the second. Reach usually moves inside a month. Anyone promising a number by a date has not seen your account yet — we would rather show you the first month and let it argue for itself.
Why is there no price list?
Because a restaurant posting three times a week and a brand running paid social across four markets are not the same job, and quoting them from the same table means someone gets overcharged. Send the scope on WhatsApp and you get one straight number, usually the same day.
