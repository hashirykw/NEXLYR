AI · Voice

AI calling agents

A chatbot catches people already on your site. A calling agent goes after the ones who filled a form and then went quiet — which, for most businesses, is where the majority of the revenue is sitting.

Speed-to-lead calling

The agent rings a new enquiry within minutes of it arriving, while the person still remembers submitting it. Answer rates from a call in the first five minutes are in a completely different league to one made the next morning.

  • Calls new leads automatically
  • Retries on no-answer
  • Leaves a voicemail when it should

Qualification & booking

It holds a real conversation — asks what they need, budget range, timeline and location — then books the ones worth your time directly into your calendar and politely closes out the ones that are not a fit.

  • Natural, interruptible speech
  • Calendar booking and reminders
  • Escalates to a human on request

Follow-up & records

It chases the quotes nobody replied to, reminds people about tomorrow’s appointment, and reduces no-shows. Every call is transcribed and tagged with an outcome so you can read exactly what was said instead of guessing.

  • Automated follow-up sequences
  • Appointment reminders
  • Full transcripts and outcomes

Best used together. The chatbot captures and qualifies on the site, the calling agent picks up the moment someone submits, and both hand the finished lead to your WhatsApp. Most businesses start with one and add the other once they have seen it work.

Questions

Before you brief us

Do I need a web app or just a website?
If the site only has to explain what you do and get people to contact you, a website is the right answer and anything more is wasted money. You need an app or portal the moment users have to log in, see something personal to them, or when you need to change content daily without calling a developer. We will tell you honestly which one you are — including when the cheaper option is the correct one.
Can you rebuild my existing site?
Yes, and it is a common request. We audit what is there, keep the copy and assets that are working, and rebuild the structure and front-end properly. If you are on WordPress and it has become slow or fragile, a hand-coded rebuild usually loads several times faster and costs less to run afterwards.
What do you need from me to start?
Your logo files if you have them, any photos or video you want used, the text for your services, and access to your domain. If some of that does not exist yet, we can handle the design side and guide you on what to shoot or write — missing assets are the single most common reason projects stall.
Will the site work on old phones?
That is a core requirement, not a bonus. Everything is tested down to 360px width and on mid-range Android over mobile data. Heavy effects like 3D and blur are deferred or reduced automatically where the device cannot carry them, and every animation respects the reduced-motion setting.
Can you add a chatbot or calling agent to a site you did not build?
Usually yes. Both are designed to sit on top of an existing site, and integration is normally a small piece of work compared to a full build. Send us the URL on WhatsApp and we will tell you straight away whether it is straightforward.
